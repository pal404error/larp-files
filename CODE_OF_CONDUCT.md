# Code of Conduct

`.larp` is a small project with a simple rule: **be decent to the people
you're building with.**

- Treat every contributor, user, and maintainer with respect. No
  harassment, no personal attacks, no slurs, no bad-faith arguments.
- Critique the code, not the person. A wrong approach is a chance to
  teach, not to dunk.
- Assume good intent until proven otherwise. Most mistakes are just
  mistakes.
- Keep it on-topic: issues and PRs are about the project, not about
  each other.

## Enforcement

Report violations to the maintainers via GitHub's private vulnerability
reporting flow (Security tab) or by opening an issue titled
"Code of Conduct report" with as little detail as you are comfortable
sharing publicly — a maintainer will follow up privately.

Violations may lead to a warning, a temporary ban from the repository,
or a permanent ban, depending on severity. Maintainers enforce this
policy in good faith and will not retaliate against anyone who reports
in good faith.
